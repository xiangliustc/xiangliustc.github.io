NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted Sep 25, 2026 First author

Q-Probe: Scaling Image Quality Assessment to High Resolution via Context-Aware Agentic Probing

8× A100 · Context-aware agentic probing for image quality assessment

  1. Agentic framework. Downsampling at high resolution drops fine detail, and cropping is often mistaken for distortion. Q-Probe is an agentic IQA framework in the Thinking with Images style, following a human “look globally, then inspect locally” process.
  2. Vista-Bench. A high-resolution, fine-grained IQA benchmark. Wavelets separate structure from texture, subtle damage is injected into semantically important regions, and Gemini-2.5 writes hierarchical labels so natural bokeh is not confused with blur artifacts.
  3. Three-stage training. Low-resolution GRPO aligns global taste. Mixed-resolution SFT uses a data flywheel of positive and negative chain-of-thought traces to remove tool-use bias. Decoupled post-RL splits the rewards for localization and scoring.
CVPR 2026 · Accepted Feb 21, 2026 First author

Look Before You Fuse: 2D-Guided Cross-Modal Alignment for Robust 3D Detection

8× 4090 · BEV 3D detection by fusing point-cloud and image features · nuScenes mAP 71.5%

  1. A 2D-prior-guided cross-modal alignment method for the spatial mismatch between LiDAR and cameras.
  2. Prior-Guided Depth Calibration (PGDC) reduces local feature misalignment. Discontinuity-Aware Geometric Fusion (DAGF) suppresses global noise.
  3. A Structure-Guided Depth Modulator (SGDM) fuses the aligned depth with image features. The method reaches SOTA on nuScenes, with mAP 71.5%.
